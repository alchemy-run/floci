    Amazon IVS (Low-Latency Streaming)

    Protocol: REST JSON (POST /<Operation>, /tags/<arn>) Endpoint: http://localhost:4566/, signed for ivs.

    Channels stream for real. Floci runs one MediaMTX media server (bluenviron/mediamtx:1.21.3-ffmpeg) for every channel, started when the first channel is created. A broadcaster publishes RTMP to the channel's ingest endpoint, rtmp://<ingestEndpoint>/app/<stream key>, where the ingest endpoint is the gateway host and floci.services.ivs.rtmp-port (1935). SRT goes to the channel's srt.endpoint (floci.services.ivs.srt-port, 9000/udp) with streamid=publish:app/<stream key> and the channel's passphrase. Only a channel's current stream key publishes; deleting the key disconnects its broadcaster. RTMPS is not served.

    A stream is LIVE from when its media arrives, HEALTHY while bytes keep arriving and STARVING after three seconds without them, and its viewer count is the number of HLS sessions playing it. Each broadcast is a stream session with its ingest configuration (codecs, resolution, profile, level, sample rate, channels) and events. EventBridge receives IVS Stream State Change (Session Created, Stream Start, Stream End, Session Ended), IVS Stream Health Change (Starvation Start and End), IVS Recording State Change and IVS Ad Break State Change events from aws.ivs.

    The playback URL, <gateway>/api/video/v1/<region>.<account>.channel.<id>.m3u8, plays the channel's HLS while it is live. A private (authorized) channel takes ?token=<JWT>: ES384-signed with the private key of one of the account's playback key pairs, with aws:channel-arn and exp, and optionally aws:access-control-allow-origin, aws:strict-origin-enforcement, aws:viewer-id and aws:viewer-session-version. Revoking a viewer's sessions stops their playback, segments included. A playback restriction policy limits the Origin that may play; countries are restricted only with floci.services.ivs.viewer-country set, as floci cannot locate viewers.

    A channel with a recording configuration is recorded to its S3 bucket in IVS's layout: ivs/v1/<account>/<channel>/<y>/<m>/<d>/<h>/<mm>/<recording id>/ with events/recording-started.json and recording-ended.json (or recording-failed.json), media/hls/master.m3u8 and byte-range-multivariant.m3u8, the source rendition's playlist.m3u8 and byte-range-variant.m3u8, and thumbnails at the configured interval and resolution (media/thumbnails/, media/latest_thumbnail/thumb.jpg). Floci does not transcode, so the recording has the one source rendition, written when the recording ends. A stream that reconnects within the configuration's reconnect window, at the same resolution, is merged into the same recording. A configuration whose bucket does not exist is CREATE_FAILED.

    PutMetadata is validated (1 KB, 5 a second per channel) and recorded on the stream's session; it is not inserted into the HLS segments. InsertAdBreak needs a live channel with an ad configuration and publishes the ad break event. Live channels cannot be updated or deleted. With floci.services.ivs.mock: true, or without Docker, channels are never live.

    Real-Time Streaming (stages)

    IVS Real-Time's API signs as ivs too and is served alongside: stages, participant tokens, encoder, storage and ingest configurations, public keys, compositions and participant replication. Stages are published to for real, through the same media server, over the two protocols IVS takes from encoders other than its broadcast SDK:

    • WHIP: a stage's endpoints.whip is <gateway>/_floci/ivsrealtime/whip. A WHIP client (OBS, ffmpeg's whip muxer, a browser) POSTs its SDP offer with a participant token as its bearer token. The token is verified as IVS does: an ES384 JWT that CreateParticipantToken issued, or one the application signed with a key it imported (ImportPublicKey, the key's ARN as kid), unexpired, for a stage that exists, of a participant not disconnected. A token without PUBLISH, or an offer without H.264 video, is refused with a PUBLISH_ERROR. WebRTC media flows on floci.services.ivs.webrtc-port (8189/udp).
    • RTMP and RTMPS: an ingest configuration's stream key publishes to the stage's endpoints.rtmp (floci.services.ivs.rtmp-port) or endpoints.rtmps (floci.services.ivs.rtmps-port, 1937, with a certificate Floci's CA issues), as rtmp(s)://<endpoint>/app/<stream key>. An RTMPS configuration refuses plain RTMP unless insecureIngest, and a key whose configuration names no stage is refused.

    A publisher joins the stage's session (started by its first participant, ended after its last) as a participant, CONNECTED and published while its media flows, with JOINED, PUBLISH_STARTED, PUBLISH_STOPPED and LEFT participant events and IVS Stage Update events (Participant Published, Unpublished, Publish Error, Replication Start and End). DisconnectParticipant disconnects a participant and refuses it from then on.

    A stage with an autoParticipantRecordingConfiguration records each publisher to its storage configuration's bucket in IVS's layout, <stage>/<session>/<participant>/<time>/: events/recording-started.json and recording-ended.json, media/hls/multivariant.m3u8 and high/playlist.m3u8 with fMP4 segments and EXT-X-PROGRAM-DATE-TIME, and thumbnails, with IVS Participant Recording State Change events. A publisher that returns within the reconnect window continues the same recording.

    StartComposition mixes the stage's publishers (a grid, or picture-in-picture) with ffmpeg at its encoder configuration's size, into a channel, which goes live as if broadcast to, and into an S3 composite recording at <stage>/<composition>/<destination>/composite/ with HLS, thumbnails and metadata files, re-laid out as publishers come and go, with IVS Composition State Change events. A composition stops itself after floci.services.ivs.composition-idle-seconds (60) without publishers, and is deleted five minutes after it stops. StartParticipantReplication pulls a publisher into another stage, where it appears as a REPLICA participant.

    The events endpoint, which IVS's broadcast SDKs use to subscribe, is not served: subscribing to a stage needs the IVS SDKs' own signaling.

    BatchGetChannel Get several channels by ARN, with per-ARN errors

    BatchStartViewerSessionRevocation Revoke several viewers' playback sessions
    CreateAdConfiguration Create an ad configuration for server-side ad insertion
    CreateChannel Create a channel and its stream key, with RTMP and SRT ingest endpoints
    CreatePlaybackRestrictionPolicy Create a policy limiting playback by origin and country
    CreateRecordingConfiguration Create an S3 recording configuration (CREATE_FAILED when the bucket is missing)
    CreateStreamKey Create a channel's stream key (one per channel)
    DeleteAdConfiguration Delete an ad configuration no channel uses
    DeleteChannel Delete a channel that is not live, and its stream keys
    DeletePlaybackKeyPair Delete a playback key pair
    DeletePlaybackRestrictionPolicy Delete a playback restriction policy no channel uses
    DeleteRecordingConfiguration Delete a recording configuration no channel uses
    DeleteStreamKey Delete a stream key, disconnecting its broadcaster
    GetAdConfiguration Get an ad configuration
    GetChannel Get a channel
    GetPlaybackKeyPair Get a playback key pair
    GetPlaybackRestrictionPolicy Get a playback restriction policy
    GetRecordingConfiguration Get a recording configuration
    GetStream Get a channel's live stream, its health and viewer count
    GetStreamKey Get a stream key and its value
    GetStreamSession Get a stream session with its ingest configuration, recording and events
    ImportPlaybackKeyPair Import a P-384 public key for signing playback tokens (3 per account)
    InsertAdBreak Start an ad break on a live channel and publish its event
    ListAdConfigurations List ad configurations
    ListChannels List channels, filtered by name, recording configuration or restriction policy
    ListPlaybackKeyPairs List playback key pairs
    ListPlaybackRestrictionPolicies List playback restriction policies
    ListRecordingConfigurations List recording configurations
    ListStreamKeys List a channel's stream keys
    ListStreamSessions List a channel's stream sessions, newest first
    ListStreams List live streams, filtered by health
    PutMetadata Validate and record timed metadata on a live stream (1 KB, 5 a second)
    StartViewerSessionRevocation Revoke a viewer's playback sessions up to a session version
    StopStream Disconnect a channel's broadcaster
    UpdateAdConfiguration Update an ad configuration
    UpdateChannel Update a channel that is not live
    UpdatePlaybackRestrictionPolicy Update a playback restriction policy
    ListTagsForResource List a resource's tags
    TagResource Add tags to a resource
    UntagResource Remove tags from a resource
    CreateEncoderConfiguration Create a composition encoder configuration (size, frame rate, bitrate)
    CreateIngestConfiguration Create an RTMP(S) ingest configuration and its stream key for a stage
    CreateParticipantToken Issue an ES384 participant token for a stage
    CreateStage Create a stage with its WHIP and RTMP(S) endpoints, participant recording and tokens
    CreateStorageConfiguration Create a storage configuration for an existing S3 bucket
    DeleteEncoderConfiguration Delete an encoder configuration no running composition uses
    DeleteIngestConfiguration Delete an ingest configuration (force disconnects a live one)
    DeletePublicKey Delete an imported public key; tokens it signed are refused
    DeleteStage Delete a stage, disconnecting its participants and failing its compositions
    DeleteStorageConfiguration Delete a storage configuration no stage or composition uses
    DisconnectParticipant Disconnect a participant and refuse it from then on
    GetComposition Get a composition and its destinations' states
    GetEncoderConfiguration Get an encoder configuration
    GetIngestConfiguration Get an ingest configuration, ACTIVE while publishing
    GetParticipant Get a participant of a session, with its protocol and recording
    GetPublicKey Get an imported public key
    GetStage Get a stage and its active session
    GetStageSession Get a stage session
    GetStorageConfiguration Get a storage configuration
    ImportPublicKey Import a P-384 public key for self-signed participant tokens (3 per account)
    ListCompositions List compositions, filtered by stage or encoder configuration
    ListEncoderConfigurations List encoder configurations
    ListIngestConfigurations List ingest configurations, filtered by stage or state
    ListParticipantEvents List a participant's events in a session
    ListParticipantReplicas List a participant's replications
    ListParticipants List a session's participants, with one filter
    ListPublicKeys List imported public keys
    ListStageSessions List a stage's sessions, newest first
    ListStages List stages
    ListStorageConfigurations List storage configurations
    RevokeConnectionToken Revoke a connection token
    StartComposition Mix a stage's publishers into a channel and/or an S3 composite recording
    StartParticipantReplication Replicate a publishing participant into another stage
    StopComposition Stop a composition, finishing its recordings
    StopParticipantReplication Stop a participant's replication
    UpdateIngestConfiguration Move an ingest configuration to another stage, or change redundant ingest
    UpdateStage Update a stage's name and participant recording
