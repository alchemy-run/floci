    AWS Elemental MediaTailor

    Protocol: REST JSON Endpoint: http://localhost:4566/playbackConfiguration, /channel/..., /sourceLocation/..., signed for mediatailor

    Floci runs MediaTailor's ad insertion and channel assembly. Players reach an account's playback host, <id>.mediatailor.<region>.localhost.floci.io, and its channel assembly host, <id>.channel-assembly.mediatailor.<region>.localhost.floci.io, on Floci's port.

    Playback configurations

    A playback configuration gets its session initialization, HLS and DASH endpoints on the playback host. Put replaces every setting it's given and clears the ones it isn't; log configuration and tags carry over. Logging, prefetch schedules and functions are kept per configuration. Deleting one deletes its prefetch schedules.

    Ad insertion

    A session starts implicitly at the HLS endpoint (/v1/master/..., with ads.<name> query parameters as player parameters) or explicitly with a POST to the session initialization endpoint (with adsParams, playerParams, availSuppression and reportingMode). Its multivariant playlist names a personalized playlist per track.

    Each media playlist is the origin's, with its ad avails filled. An avail starts at EXT-X-CUE-OUT or at an EXT-X-DATERANGE with SCTE35-OUT, and lasts its duration or until EXT-X-CUE-IN. MediaTailor requests the ad decision server, filling its dynamic variables ([session.*], [avail.*], [player_params.*], through configuration aliases), and follows VAST wrappers. Then it places the linear ads that fit the avail, with the bumpers, and the slate for the rest. When there's no slate and the unfilled time passes the personalization threshold, the content plays instead. Ads replace the content they cover, between discontinuity tags. Avails a session joins midway aren't filled, and avail suppression keeps avails behind or after the live edge unfilled, or partly filled with PARTIAL_AVAIL. A VOD playlist without markers gets the ad decision server's VMAP breaks (start, times, percentages, end), or its VAST ads as a pre-roll. A live session gets the live pre-roll. Ad markers pass through when the manifest processing rules ask. Content and ad segment URLs take the CDN prefixes.

    Ads are conditioned for each track. With TRANSCODE (the default), ffmpeg transcodes the best MP4 media file to the track's size, bitrate and segment duration, as TS or as fragmented MP4 with an init section, like the content. With NONE, an HLS media file is used as it is. A manifest request waits up to transcode-wait-millis for its ads; an ad not ready by then is left out of that avail and keeps transcoding for later sessions. Transcoding needs Docker: ffmpeg runs in the image Kinesis Video Streams transcodes with.

    The playback host serves ad segments. With server-side reporting, it fires each ad's impressions and its start, quartile and complete tracking events as their segments are requested. With client-side reporting, the session's tracking URL lists the avails, ads and beacons instead. Prefetch schedules request ads for their retrieval window's dynamic variables, and avails in the consumption window that match their criteria use those ads. Recurring schedules retrieve again after each avail. When a configuration's log percentage samples a session, its ad decision server and manifest events go to CloudWatch Logs, in MediaTailor/AdDecisionServerInteractions and MediaTailor/ManifestService.

    DASH manifests come from the origin with their segment URLs resolved and a Location for dynamic ones; ads aren't inserted into them.

    Channel assembly

    Source locations name the base URL their VOD and live sources' packages are read from. A program's duration is its VOD source's, read from its manifest and clipped. A LOOP channel repeats its programs, in the order their relative transitions set, from when it started. A LINEAR channel plays them at their absolute times, or after or before the program they're relative to, with the filler slate in the gaps. A program's ad breaks play their slate at their offsets.

    While a channel runs, each HLS output serves a sliding window of the schedule with program date times. Ad breaks are marked with EXT-X-DATERANGE SCTE-35 splice inserts, EXT-X-CUE-OUT/EXT-X-CUE-IN, or both, as the output's ad markup types ask, so a playback configuration with the output as its source fills them. aws.time_delay shifts the window back on channels with time-shifted viewing. DASH outputs answer 501. GetChannelSchedule lists programs, filler slates, alternate media for an audience, and the ad breaks. Sources that can't be read raise alerts on the program. AS_RUN logs go to MediaTailor/Channel/AsRunLog. A channel is updated and deleted only while stopped, and a source in use can't be deleted.

    Configuration

    floci:
  services:
    media-tailor:
      enabled: true
      request-timeout-millis: 10000   # origin, ad decision server and beacon requests
      transcode-wait-millis: 30000    # how long a manifest request waits for its ads
      session-idle-seconds: 600       # how long a session lasts after its last request

    CreateChannel Create a channel with an HLS or DASH playback URL per output
    CreateLiveSource Create live source
    CreatePrefetchSchedule Prefetch ads for matching avails
    CreateProgram Schedule a program; its duration is read from its source
    CreateSourceLocation Create source location
    CreateVodSource Create VOD source
    DeleteChannel Delete a stopped channel and its programs
    DeleteChannelPolicy Delete channel policy
    DeleteFunction Delete a function no configuration uses
    DeleteLiveSource Delete a live source no program uses
    DeletePlaybackConfiguration Delete a configuration and its prefetch schedules
    DeletePrefetchSchedule Delete prefetch schedule
    DeleteProgram Delete program
    DeleteSourceLocation Delete a source location without sources
    DeleteVodSource Delete a VOD source no program or slate uses
    DescribeChannel Describe channel
    DescribeLiveSource Describe live source
    DescribeProgram Describe a program with its scheduled start
    DescribeSourceLocation Describe source location
    DescribeVodSource Describe a VOD source with its ad break opportunities
    GetChannelPolicy Get channel policy
    GetChannelSchedule A channel's upcoming programs, filler slates and ad breaks
    GetFunction Get function
    GetPlaybackConfiguration Get a configuration with its endpoints

    StopChannel Stop channel

    UpdateChannel Update a stopped channel
