    AWS Deadline Cloud

    Protocol: REST JSON Endpoint: http://localhost:4566/ with Deadline Cloud's paths (/2023-10-12/farms, /2023-10-12/monitors, /2023-10-12/license-endpoints, /2023-10-12/tags/{resourceArn}, ...), signed for deadline.

    Floci runs Deadline Cloud farms end to end: queues, fleets, storage profiles, budgets, limits and their associations, jobs submitted from Open Job Description templates with their steps and tasks, and the scheduling protocol customer-managed fleet workers speak, so the Deadline worker agent can run jobs against floci.

    Farms, queues and fleets

    A farm refuses deletion while it holds queues, fleets, storage profiles, budgets, limits or associations. A queue or fleet association must be stopped (STOP_SCHEDULING_AND_COMPLETE_TASKS or STOP_SCHEDULING_AND_CANCEL_TASKS) and settle to STOPPED before it is deleted, and a storage profile a queue allows or a fleet uses cannot be deleted. Creating a queue or fleet creates its log group under /aws/deadline/<farmId>/, which, as on AWS, remains after the farm is deleted.

    A fleet's capabilities derive from its configuration: amount.worker.vcpu, amount.worker.memory, GPU amounts, attr.worker.os.family and attr.worker.cpu.arch, plus custom amounts and attributes. A queue whose defaultBudgetAction is not NONE is SCHEDULING_BLOCKED until a budget tracks it. Budgets keep their schedule (at most 120 days) and actions; floci bills nothing for worker time, so their usage stays at zero.

    Jobs

    CreateJob parses the template (JSON or YAML) and validates it as jobtemplate-2023-09: step names, scripts, task parameter definitions, environments and acyclic step dependencies. Submitted parameters are checked against the template's definitions (types, bounds, allowed values) with defaults filled in, and the job's name resolves its {{Param.X}} references. Each step's parameter space expands into tasks: integer ranges such as 1-100:2, lists, CHUNK[INT] chunks, and combination expressions (A * B products, (A, B) associations).

    Tasks of a step whose dependencies have not all succeeded are PENDING; others are READY, or NOT_COMPATIBLE when the queue's fleets cannot meet the step's host requirements. Run statuses roll up into each step and job, which publish Job Lifecycle Status Change, Job Run Status Change, Step Run Status Change and Task Run Status Change events from aws.deadline to the default event bus. Jobs, steps and tasks are requeued, suspended, failed, succeeded or canceled through their update operations; a task in flight is interrupted and settles when its worker reports.

    Workers

    A worker of a customer-managed fleet registers with CreateWorker, starts with UpdateWorker and its capabilities, and polls UpdateWorkerSchedule. Each poll applies the session actions the worker reports and assigns work: the highest-priority ready task (oldest job first) of a queue its fleet is actively associated with, whose host requirements its capabilities meet and whose queue limits have room. The task runs in a session that syncs job attachments and enters the queue's, job's and step's environments, takes further ready tasks of the same step, then exits its environments in reverse order and ends. A failed task retries up to the job's maxRetriesPerTask; a job that reaches maxFailedTasksCount failed tasks cancels the rest. BatchGetJobEntity serves the job, step, environment and attachment details the agent needs, with path mapping rules between the job's and the fleet's storage profiles, and AssumeFleetRoleForWorker and AssumeQueueRoleForWorker vend the fleet's and queue's role credentials.

    Service-managed fleets keep their configuration and capabilities, but floci launches no EC2 workers for them: their tasks wait for a worker, and they have no persistent volumes.

    Monitors and licensing

    A monitor creates an IAM Identity Center application in the given instance (through floci's SSO Admin service) and serves at https://<subdomain>.<region>.deadlinecloud.amazonaws.com. License endpoints check their VPC and serve the usage-based licensing products of ListAvailableMeteredProducts.

    Configuration

    floci:
  services:
    deadline:
      enabled: true
      transition-millis: 2000   # how long a fleet, job update, license endpoint or statistics aggregation stays in progress
